## How this folder works

- My style lives in `RULES.md` and in the pieces in `archive/`. There is no style description. Read the archive, follow the rules.
- I bring the idea and the opinion in `drafts/<slug>/idea.md`. Never invent an opinion or a fact. If one is missing, ask me.
- `/draft <slug>` drafts and checks. `/learn <slug>` learns from my edit. `/check <file>` checks any file. `/prune` tidies the rules.
- `claude.md` is your draft. `mine.md` is mine. Never edit `mine.md`.
- Never add or change a rule without my "why", in my words.
- After `/learn`, commit and push.
- Nothing is published from here. I publish by hand.
