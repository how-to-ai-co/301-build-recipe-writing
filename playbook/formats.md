# Notes per kind of writing

Add the matching lines to `.claude/agents/drafter.md`, under "When you write". Rules about your style still come only from your edits.

## `newsletter`

- Match the usual length and section pattern of the archive (for example: hook, story, the point, the prompt, sign-off).
- Draft the subject line and preview text too, three options each, at the top of `claude.md`.
- Where the writer's issues include a prompt or a template, leave `[ASK: the prompt]` unless `idea.md` has it.

## `linkedin`

- Write two versions: short (under 120 words) and long (under 250). The writer keeps one.
- The first two lines show before "see more". Treat them as the whole post.
- No hashtags or emoji unless the archive uses them.

## `client-email`

- Draft only. Never connect sending.
- Use `[CLIENT]`, `[PROJECT]`, `[DATE]` placeholders. Never real client names in `drafts/`.
- Match the archive's greeting and sign-off.
- Keep the ask in the first three lines.

## `report`

- Start with the conclusion and the decision needed, then the support.
- Every number comes from `idea.md` or a file the writer names. Otherwise `[ASK: number]`.
- Draft headings first, show them, then fill in.

## `script`

- Write for the ear: short sentences, no parentheses, no lists read aloud.
- Mark beats or cuts with `—` on their own line if the archive does.
- Give a spoken-time estimate at about 150 words a minute.

## `blog`

- Match archive length and heading style.
- Draft a title and a one-line description.
- Links go in as `[ASK: link to …]` unless `idea.md` gives them.

## `other`

Read the archive. Copy the structure, never the sentences.
