# Safety

## Private by default

- The GitHub repository is **private**. Check the Private label on its page after every new setup.
- Only you are a collaborator. Do not add anyone unless you mean to share every piece and every rule.
- If you connect GitHub to Claude, allow only the `writing-room` repository.

## Client and confidential writing

Your archive teaches style. It does not need the secrets.

- Before saving client emails, reports or memos, replace client names, people's names, prices and figures with `[CLIENT]`, `[NAME]`, `[AMOUNT]`. Claude can do it; check the result.
- Leave out anything under an NDA (a non-disclosure agreement) or marked confidential, unless you are sure it is yours to store.
- If your company has an AI policy, follow it. Some do not allow work documents in personal accounts at all.

## Claude and your data

On Claude Pro, Max and Ultra plans, check Settings, Privacy, and decide whether to allow your chats to be used to improve models. On Team and Enterprise plans this is off by default. **(check before class: setting name and defaults)**

## Nothing publishes itself

This build drafts and checks. It never posts, publishes or sends. You copy the final text into Substack, LinkedIn, Gmail or wherever, by hand. Keep it that way.

## Facts

The drafter is told never to add a fact, number, quote or story that is not in `idea.md`. It can still get one wrong. Read every number and name before you publish.

## End-of-class checklist

Go through it with Claude, one line at a time.

- [ ] `~/Documents/writing-room/` exists and has `CLAUDE.md`, `RULES.md`, `archive/`, `drafts/`, `edits/`.
- [ ] `CLAUDE.md` is under 200 words and does not describe my style.
- [ ] Every rule in `RULES.md` has my words in its "Why" line.
- [ ] The checker ran on one of my own archive pieces and flagged few or no lines.
- [ ] No client names, prices or confidential material in the archive.
- [ ] The GitHub repository shows **Private**, and I am the only collaborator.
- [ ] I ran `/draft`, edited `mine.md`, and ran `/learn` on one real piece.
- [ ] The latest `/learn` is pushed (the repository page shows today's commit).
- [ ] I know the weekly routine and when to run `/prune`.
