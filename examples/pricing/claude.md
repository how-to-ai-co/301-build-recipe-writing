You are charging half what you should, and the hour is the reason.

Eight of the ten freelancers I know charge by the hour. It feels fair and simple. It also caps your income at the hours in your week.

The client is not really paying for your time. They are paying for the outcome you deliver.

Last year, I decided to double my rate. It was a scary decision. I was worried that I would lose clients. In the end, I lost one client out of nine, and the other eight stayed with me.

Here's the thing: you are not selling hours. You are selling results.

This week, pick one client and quote the outcome, not the hours.
