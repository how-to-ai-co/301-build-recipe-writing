# My rules

## Rules

### R1. Open with the claim. Never with context or "In today's…".
- Why (my words): "Nobody reads past a warm-up. Start where it hurts."
- Before: "In today's issue, I want to talk about pricing."
- After: "You are charging half what you should."
- From: edits/blind-rates.md
- Caught: 1

### R2. One idea per paragraph. Three lines at most.
- Why (my words): "People read me on their phone, half-awake."
- Before: (a six-line paragraph covering pricing and contracts)
- After: (two paragraphs, two lines each)
- From: edits/blind-contracts.md
- Caught: 0

### R3. Use a number instead of "many", "a lot", "significant".
- Why (my words): "Vague words are where I'm hiding that I don't know."
- Before: "Many freelancers struggle with pricing."
- After: "Eight of the ten freelancers I know charge by the hour."
- From: edits/blind-rates.md
- Caught: 1

### R4. Never start a sentence or a title with "No".
- Why (my words): "It sounds like I'm arguing with someone who isn't there."
- Before: "No one tells you this when you start."
- After: "Nobody warned me."
- From: edits/blind-clients.md
- Caught: 1

### R5. Keep sentences under 20 words.
- Why (my words): "If I need a breath, it's two sentences."
- Before: (a 34-word sentence about scope)
- After: (three sentences)
- From: edits/blind-contracts.md
- Caught: 0

### R6. End on the action, not a summary.
- Why (my words): "They already read it. Tell them what to do Monday."
- Before: "In conclusion, it might be worth considering…"
- After: "This week, pick one client and quote the outcome."
- From: edits/blind-rates.md
- Caught: 1

### R7. Cut the feelings. Keep the facts and let the number carry it.
- Why (my words): "Readers don't need my nerves. The number is the drama."
- Before: "It was a scary decision. I was worried that I would lose clients."
- After: "I lost one client out of nine. One."
- From: edits/2026-11-04-pricing.md
- Caught: 0

### R8. Say what the client actually wants, not "outcome", "value" or "results".
- Why (my words): "'Outcome' is a word from a slide. Say what they actually want."
- Before: "They are paying for the outcome you deliver."
- After: "They want the new site live before their launch."
- From: edits/2026-11-04-pricing.md
- Caught: 0

### R9. No announcer lines ("Here's the thing", "The truth is"). Say the line.
- Why (my words): "If the line is good it doesn't need a drumroll."
- Before: "Here's the thing: you are not selling hours. You are selling results."
- After: "You are not selling hours."
- From: edits/2026-11-04-pricing.md
- Caught: 0
