In today's issue, I want to talk about pricing, one of the most important decisions any freelancer makes.

Many freelancers struggle with pricing. They charge by the hour because it feels fair and simple. However, hourly pricing can significantly limit your income over time.

No one tells you this when you start out, but the client is not really paying for your time. They are paying for the outcome you deliver.

Last year, I decided to double my rate. It was a scary decision. I was worried that I would lose a lot of clients. In the end, I lost only one client out of nine, and the other eight stayed with me.

Here's the thing: you are not selling hours. You are selling results.

In conclusion, if you want to grow your freelance business, it might be worth considering moving away from hourly pricing and towards outcome-based pricing.
